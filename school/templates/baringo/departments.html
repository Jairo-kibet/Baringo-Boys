Academic Excellence

Academics & Departments

Our academic departments provide a supportive and stimulating learning environment where learners develop knowledge, practical skills, creativity and critical thinking. Through the Competency-Based Education framework, our departments deliver learning experiences that prepare students for higher education and the challenges of the modern world.

Our Academic Structure

Knowledge, Skills and Practical Learning

Our departments are organized around five major academic areas: Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, Applied & Technical Studies and Languages. Together, they support the development of learners across the Senior School curriculum and its learning pathways.
